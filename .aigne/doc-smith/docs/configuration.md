# Configuration

Tokio is designed to be flexible and efficient, allowing you to tailor it to your specific application's needs. Proper configuration can significantly impact your project's compile time, binary size, and available features. This section guides you through the key aspects of configuring Tokio, from selecting feature flags to understanding platform-specific behavior and opting into experimental APIs.
